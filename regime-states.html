Regime Classification

What trending, ranging & uncertain actually mean.

Quasar classifies every 4-hour candle into one of three regime states. Each state triggers a different strategy configuration.

🟣

State 1: Trending

ADX > 25. Clear directional movement detected across multiple indicators. Momentum strategies are activated.

Quasar allocates 70% weight to momentum indicators (MACD direction, RSI trend confirmation). Reversion signals are suppressed to avoid premature counter-trend entries.

Signal frequency: Normal
Position size: Standard
Momentum weight: 70%
🔵

State 2: Ranging

ADX < 20. Price oscillating between support and resistance levels. Mean reversion strategies are activated.

Quasar allocates 60% weight to reversion indicators (Bollinger Band boundaries, RSI overbought/oversold). Momentum signals are dampened.

Signal frequency: Normal
Position size: Reduced (-25%)
Reversion weight: 60%
⚪

State 3: Uncertain

Mixed signals. ADX between 20–25. Indicators disagree on market direction. No clear regime detected.

Quasar reduces all signal frequency by 50% and sets position sizes to minimum. Capital protection mode is active until a clear regime re-emerges.

Signal frequency: -50%
Position size: Minimum
Mode: Capital protection
Regime Transitions

How Quasar handles regime shifts

Regime transitions are the most critical moments in adaptive trading. When the market shifts from trending to ranging (or vice versa), Quasar follows a structured transition protocol:

  • Confirmation period: A regime change requires 2 consecutive 4-hour candles confirming the new state before full weight reallocation.
  • Gradual weight shift: Indicator weights transition over 2 candles rather than switching instantly, reducing whipsaw risk.
  • Open position management: Existing positions maintain their original stop loss and take profit levels. Only new signals use updated weights.
  • Uncertain buffer: The "uncertain" state acts as a buffer between trending and ranging, preventing rapid oscillation between strategies.

Despite these safeguards, regime misclassification remains a core risk of adaptive systems. Quasar may incorrectly classify a trending market as ranging during volatile transitions, leading to strategy misalignment. Not financial advice.

Indicators

The classification inputs

ADX (14)

The Average Directional Index measures trend strength regardless of direction. ADX > 25 confirms a trend; ADX < 20 confirms ranging. The 20–25 zone is the "uncertain" buffer.

Primary regime classifier
Bollinger Band Width

Measures volatility expansion and contraction. Expanding bands support trending classification; contracting bands support ranging classification.

Volatility confirmation
RSI (14)

Evaluates directional momentum. Persistent readings above 55 or below 45 confirm trend direction. Oscillation between 40–60 supports ranging.

Momentum state
MACD Ensemble

Cross-references MACD histogram direction, signal line relationship, and zero-line position. Agreement across all three sub-components strengthens regime classification.
