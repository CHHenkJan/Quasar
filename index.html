The Core Problem

Every fixed strategy has a regime where it fails. Quasar adapts.

A pure momentum strategy thrives when markets trend — and destroys accounts when markets range. A pure mean reversion strategy works in ranging markets — and bleeds continuously when markets trend.

Most traders and bots use one or the other. They work for a while, then fail. The trader assumes the strategy is broken. Often, the market regime just changed.

Quasar solves this with a real-time regime classifier. At every 4-hour candle, Quasar evaluates:

  • ADX strength (is there a trend?)
  • Bollinger Band width (is volatility expanding or contracting?)
  • RSI momentum state (is price making directional progress?)
  • MACD ensemble agreement (are indicators aligned?)

Based on this evaluation, Quasar classifies the current regime and adjusts its indicator ensemble weights accordingly. In trending conditions, momentum factors dominate. In ranging conditions, reversion factors dominate. In uncertain conditions, Quasar reduces signal frequency and position size.

This is not magic. It is adaptive systematic trading.
