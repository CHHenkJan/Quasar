Methodology

Adaptive ML Methodology

How Quasar detects regime shifts and adapts its strategy in real-time.

Pipeline

The four-stage adaptive pipeline

01

Data Ingestion

Every 4-hour candle, Quasar ingests OHLCV data for BTC, ETH, and selected altcoins across supported exchanges.

02

Regime Classification

ADX, Bollinger Band width, RSI, and MACD signals are evaluated to classify the current market regime as trending, ranging, or uncertain.

03

Ensemble Weighting

Based on the detected regime, Quasar dynamically adjusts the weight of each indicator in its 5-factor ensemble model.

04

Signal Generation

Only signals with a confidence score above 75 are emitted. Each signal includes regime state, confidence, and active weights.

Regime Classifier

How regime classification works

At the core of Quasar is a multi-factor regime classifier. Unlike traditional systems that use a single indicator to determine market state, Quasar combines four independent measures:

  • ADX (Average Directional Index): Measures trend strength. Above 25 signals a strong trend; below 20 signals a ranging market.
  • Bollinger Band Width: Tracks volatility expansion and contraction. Expanding bands confirm trends; contracting bands suggest consolidation.
  • RSI (14-period): Evaluates directional momentum. Persistent readings above 55 or below 45 confirm trend direction.
  • MACD Ensemble: Cross-references MACD histogram direction, signal line relationship, and zero-line position for consensus.

When at least 3 of 4 factors agree on a regime, Quasar classifies with high confidence. When factors disagree, the system enters the "uncertain" state and reduces exposure automatically.

Dynamic Weights

Adaptive ensemble weighting

Once a regime is classified, Quasar adjusts the influence of each indicator in its signal generation ensemble:

In trending regimes: Momentum indicators (MACD, RSI direction) receive 70% of the total weight. Mean reversion signals (Bollinger Band bounce, RSI overbought/oversold) are suppressed to prevent premature exits.

In ranging regimes: Reversion indicators receive 60% weight. Momentum signals are dampened. Position sizes are reduced by 25% to account for the inherently lower signal quality in choppy conditions.

In uncertain regimes: All signal frequency is reduced by 50%. Position sizes are set to minimum. Quasar prioritizes capital preservation until a clear regime re-emerges.

Adaptive weighting does not guarantee better performance. Regime misclassification can lead to strategy misalignment and losses. Not financial advice.

Confidence Scoring

Quality over quantity

Every potential signal passes through Quasar's confidence scoring system before emission. The confidence score (0–100) reflects:

  • Regime classification agreement (how many factors aligned)
  • Individual indicator signal strength
  • Volume confirmation (is trading volume supporting the signal?)
  • Historical pattern similarity (has this setup performed well historically?)

Signals below 75 are suppressed entirely — they never reach the subscriber. This means Quasar emits 40–80 signals per month rather than hundreds, but each signal represents a higher-quality setup.
