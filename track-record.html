Expectations

What to expect from an adaptive system

  • Performance will vary significantly between market regimes — this is by design
  • In uncertain regimes, Quasar will trade less and returns may be flat or negative
  • Regime transitions are the highest-risk periods for the system
  • The confidence filter means fewer trades but higher average signal quality
  • Drawdowns are expected — no system avoids them entirely

Unlike fixed strategies, adaptive strategies change their behavior based on detected market conditions. Users should not extrapolate performance from one regime to another. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
